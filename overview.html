Can someone send email pretending to be your business?

For most small businesses, yes. Criminals send fake invoices and payment-change requests that look exactly like they came from you. Your customers pay the wrong account, and your name takes the hit. The settings that stop this are simple, but most businesses have never had them set up. Since 2024, Google and Yahoo also expect them, so missing them can push your real emails into spam.

WebsiteWhether your certificate is valid, your browser protections are on and your WordPress is up to date.
